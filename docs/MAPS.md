# The five race maps

Every map is a full **2 × 2 km** race environment built in Blender. Each one has:

* a sculpted terrain
* a closed race lap with bridges, tunnels, kerbs and barriers
* **3–4 shortcuts (most of them hidden)**
* the kit landmarks placed in the landscape
* gameplay data: checkpoints, start grid, card pickups, boost pads and an AI racing line

**No empty edges.** A backdrop continues every landscape from the map edge to the horizon (about 24 km). Riyadh's lit city sprawls on with its tower corridor, AlUla's massifs roll on, the Red Sea and the Hijaz mountains frame Jeddah, the Sarawat ranges surround Asir, and The Line runs off to both horizons.

**Alive.** Every map has a race-day crowd. Spectators stand behind fences with flags, and the fan zones have gazebos, food trucks and car parks. There are camera towers, marshal posts, sponsor boards and a TV helicopter. Every tight corner has red-and-white chevrons, with tyre walls and braking boards.

On top of that, each map has local life:

* **Riyadh:** traffic and mosques, plus picnics in the wadi.
* **AlUla:** Bedouin camps and camel caravans.
* **Jeddah:** dhows, fishing boats and families on the beach.
* **Asir:** goat herds and shepherds, plus viewpoints.
* **Future City:** people in The Line's park, yachts and air taxis.

The maps use the concept of each place from the reference photos. They are not replicas.

## Riyadh Nights — ليالي الرياض — *Olaya Interchange to Wadi Hanifa*

![RiyadhNights](previews/maps/RiyadhNights_1.jpg)

**Concept.** Golden hour turning to night over a flat desert city of highways, flyovers with loop ramps and the elevated Metro, with KAFD and Kingdom Centre rising out of it. The lap then drops off the Tuwaiq limestone escarpment into the green Wadi Hanifa and climbs back through the mud-brick ruins of Diriyah (At-Turaif).

**The lap (4.36 km, 18 checkpoints):**

1. Start on **King Fahd Road** (palms, gantry signs, a canyon of towers, Kingdom Centre on the right)
2. Up the **flyover** and round a descending **270° loop ramp** that passes *under* the flyover
3. **KAFD chicane** between the towers
4. **Escarpment cliff road** with a hairpin, down the stepped limestone ledges
5. **Wadi Hanifa** floor: palm groves, the lake, a wide meander
6. Climb out to **Diriyah**: cobbled S-bends between mud-brick walls, Masmak-style fort
7. Sweeping last corner past the stadium back onto King Fahd Road

## AlUla — العُلا — *Hegra Canyon Rally*

![AlUla](previews/maps/AlUla_1.jpg)

**Concept.** A golden sand valley winding between towering, honeycomb-eroded sandstone massifs. The landmarks sit in the landscape: Maraya reflecting the canyon, Qasr al-Farid alone on the plain, Hegra tombs carved into a cliff, a land-art sand labyrinth with a palm at its centre, and Elephant Rock lit by lanterns at dusk.

**The lap (4.24 km, 17 checkpoints):**

1. Start at the **Maraya** mirror building, packed-sand rally road with berms
2. Across the open plain past **Qasr al-Farid**
3. Under the **Rainbow Rock** arch into the **Grand Canyon S-bends** between cliffs
4. Round the **sand labyrinth**
5. Along the **Hegra tomb cliff**
6. Round the **giant dune**
7. Through the **Elephant Rock lantern lounge**, then the southern plain back to Maraya

## Jeddah — جدة — *Corniche & Al-Balad*

![JeddahCorniche](previews/maps/JeddahCorniche_1.jpg)

**Concept.** Jeddah from the sea in: the Corniche with the floating mosque, the curved seawall, palms and white railings, then the old town of Al-Balad with string lights over narrow lanes and teal/brown rawasheen houses. A lagoon cuts in from the Red Sea.

**The lap (4.45 km, 18 checkpoints):**

5. **Nassif House** plaza

![AsirMountains](previews/maps/AsirMountains_1.jpg)

## Future City — NEOM — *The Line Circuit*

![FutureCity](previews/maps/FutureCity_1.jpg)

**Concept.** No reference photos for this one, so it follows the NEOM idea: The Line, a 500 m tall mirrored megastructure running dead straight through desert and mountains with a green, terraced canyon inside; Oxagon, an octagonal port city floating on the Red Sea; maglev lines, solar trees and wind helixes. Blue hour, everything glowing.

**The lap (4.83 km, 20 checkpoints):**

1. Start **inside The Line**: glow road, terraces and sky bridges overhead
2. Canyon **chicanes** under the Skyway
3. Out through a **portal** in The Line's south wall
4. A big **banked curve** (15°) wrapped round a granite mountain
5. **Futuristic bridge** over a fjord-like gorge
6. Bridge out to **Oxagon** and once round the octagon
7. North up the **mountain valley** S-bends beside the maglev

## Building a map in Unreal

1. Run `unreal/import_assets.py` once (textures, materials, kits, cars).
2. **File → New Level → Empty Level**, save it (e.g. `/Game/SaharaRacers/Maps/L_AsirMountains`).
3. Output Log (Python): `exec(open(r"C:/path/to/game/unreal/build_map.py").read())`, then `build("AsirMountains")`.

`build()` imports `assets/maps/<Map>/` and builds everything in the level:
* terrain tiles, road chunks and water
* every prop in `layout.json`
* the start grid, checkpoint triggers, card pickups, boost pads and waypoints for the racing line and the shortcuts
* the lighting preset

All actors are tagged (`SR_Checkpoint`, `SR_CP_<n>`, `SR_Grid_<n>`, `SR_CardPickup`, `SR_BoostPad`, `SR_Line_<n>`, `SR_Shortcut`, `SR_Hidden`, …), so your Blueprints can find them with *Get All Actors With Tag*. To spawn your own pickup or checkpoint Blueprints instead of the marker meshes, set `ITEM_CLASSES` at the top of the script.

**Landscape instead of mesh terrain.** `build("AlUla", terrain="none")`, then *Landscape mode → New → Import from File* with `assets/maps/<Map>/landscape/H_<Map>.png`. Use the location and scale from `landscape.json`; `landscape_help("AlUla")` prints them. Weightmaps for the 4 paint layers are next to it.

## Files per map (`assets/maps/<Map>/`)

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `SM_<Map>_Terrain_<r>_<c>.fbx` | 4×4 Nanite terrain tiles; vertex colour = layer blend (M_SR_TerrainBlend) |
| `SM_<Map>_Track_NN.fbx`, `SM_<Map>_Shortcut_*.fbx` | road chunks: surface, shoulders, kerbs, barriers, bridges, tunnels |
| `SM_<Map>_Water.fbx` | sea / lake plane (if the map has water) |
| `props/` | map-specific landmarks (The Line portal, Oxagon, floating mosque, sand labyrinth, cable car, …) |
| `layout.json` | every placed prop: kit, asset, Unreal location (cm), yaw, scale |
| `track.json` | racing line (every 5 m: position, yaw, width, bank, air/tunnel/bridge), shortcuts with their savings, checkpoints, 8-car grid, pickups and boosts, all in Unreal units |
| `landscape/` | 2017×2017 16-bit heightmap, 4 weightmaps, `landscape.json` |
| `minimap.png` | top-down map (white = lap, orange = shortcut, magenta = hidden shortcut) |

## Changing a map

Each map is one Python file in `blender/maps/` (`riyadh.py`, `alula.py`, `jeddah.py`, `asir.py`, `future.py`):

* `make_terrain()` sculpts the heightfield.
* `tracks()` lists the control points of the lap and the shortcuts: width, height, banking, barrier style, surface, and tags (tunnel, gap = jump, bridge).
* `dress()` places the props.

Rebuild with:

```
python blender/maps/asir.py --layout     # quick: just the minimap -> /tmp/claude-0/asir_layout.png
python blender/maps/asir.py              # full build + exports + preview renders (SR_PREVIEW=1 for fast renders)
python blender/maps/make_maps_doc.py     # refresh this page
```

The shared engine is in `blender/lib/`:

* `terrain.py`: heightfield, noise, carving, Landscape and mesh export.
* `track.py`: splines, road, bridge and tunnel meshes, gameplay JSON, minimap.
* `mapkit.py`: prop placement, export and previews.
